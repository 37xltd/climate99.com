Climate99 publishes national context only when a country has at least four usable indicators. It does not turn national statistics into city weather, forecasts or personal-risk claims.

Publication rule

Every value keeps its own observation year. A page is omitted if fewer than four measures are available. CO₂ per person is calculated only when emissions and population observations are no more than two years apart.

World Bank country indicators

Carbon dioxide emissions, renewable-energy share, total population, urban population share, freshwater stress, historic climate-disaster exposure and forest area.

Known limits

Country averages conceal regional differences. Indicators update at different times. Historic disaster exposure is explicitly an average for 1990–2009. Climate99 is not a live warning system and should not be used for emergency, health, insurance or personal-exposure decisions.
